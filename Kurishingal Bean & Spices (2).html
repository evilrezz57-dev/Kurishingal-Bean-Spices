Purity & Precision

Our Farm-to-Cup Process

Every step is meticulously monitored to guarantee unmatched freshness and rich aromas.

01

Selective Handpicking

Only ripe red coffee cherries and vibrant green cardamom pods are picked manually by experienced harvesters.

02

Natural Sun Curing

Beans and spices are naturally dried on raised bamboo beds under mountain sunlight to retain innate sugars.

03

Triple Sorting & Grading

Electronic and optical sorting removes defectives, leaving only Grade-A uniform size and density beans.

04

Fresh Roasted & Sealed

Roasting occurs on order with nitrogen-flushed, one-way valve packaging to ensure peak flavor preservation.
